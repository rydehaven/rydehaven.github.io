First launch

Mac won't open it?

The first time you open Rydehaven, your Mac may say it "cannot be opened because the developer cannot be verified". That's normal for new apps. You only need to do this once.

1 Open Anyway

  1. Make sure Rydehaven is in your Applications folder, not still in the download window. If the download window is open, eject it.
  2. Open Rydehaven from Applications once, and click Done on the warning.
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  4. Open Rydehaven again and click Open.

2 If that doesn't work

  1. Open Terminal: press Command + Space, type Terminal, press Return.
  2. Copy this line, paste it into Terminal and press Return:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Rydehaven.app
  3. When it goes to a new line with no message, open Rydehaven from Applications.

On Windows? If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. Still stuck? Email rydehaven.contact@gmail.com.

FAQ

Questions

What do I need?

A bike on a smart trainer with Bluetooth, and a Mac or Windows computer with Bluetooth. A heart-rate strap is optional. Your trainer pairs inside the app.

Which trainers work?

Most Bluetooth smart trainers from recent years that use the standard fitness machine protocol (FTMS), including Wahoo KICKR, Tacx, Elite and Saris. Bluetooth power meters work for power and cadence. ANT+ isn't supported yet.

Can I race other riders?

Yes. Join a race and you line up with everyone else who joined, then race them to the line.

Can I play on my iPhone or iPad?

Riding needs the Rydehaven app on a Mac or PC. On your phone, use Rydehaven Remote for live stats, the map, the camera and emotes while you ride, plus races and chat.

Why does my computer warn me when I open it?

Your computer double-checks new apps the first time you open them. Follow Mac won't open it? above; you only do it once.

The trainer won't show up when I pair

Pedal to wake it, and close any other training or phone app that's already connected, because a trainer only talks to one app at a time. Then click Pair again.

Can I host a race?

Yes. Email rydehaven.contact@gmail.com with the course, how many laps, the day and time, and who it's for.

How do I get in touch?

Email rydehaven.contact@gmail.com with questions, feedback or anything that isn't working.
